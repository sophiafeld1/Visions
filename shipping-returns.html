Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Orders ship within 3–5 business days after payment is confirmed. Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on your delivery address within the United States.

Returns & Refunds

Because most Visions pieces are one-of-a-kind and handmade, all sales are final unless an item arrives damaged or not as described. If there is an issue with your order, contact us within 7 days of delivery at @visionssclothing and we will work with you on a replacement or refund.

Exchanges

Exchanges are not available since all items are one-of-a-kind.
